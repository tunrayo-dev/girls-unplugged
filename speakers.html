Why It Matters

Sometimes, one conversation can change how you see yourself.

Girls deserve access to people who are willing to share the honest parts of their journeys — not just the polished results.

Honest conversations

We create room for real questions, real experiences and conversations that go beyond surface-level advice.

New perspectives

Hearing different stories can help girls discover possibilities they may never have considered before.

Practical growth

Our conversations are designed to leave girls with ideas, lessons and practical steps they can carry into everyday life.

Room to dream

We want girls to leave conversations feeling encouraged to explore, create, lead and become more of who they are.

The Right Fit

What we look for in a guest speaker.

You don't have to have a perfect story or a long list of titles. What matters is what you can genuinely bring into the room.

Something real to share

An experience, lesson, perspective or story that can help girls learn and reflect.

Willingness to listen

We value people who are open to hearing girls' questions, experiences and perspectives too.

A community mindset

Our conversations are about connection, not performance. We want guests who care about creating meaningful spaces.

Something girls can carry forward

Whether it's an idea, practical advice or a new perspective, we want every conversation to leave something useful behind.
