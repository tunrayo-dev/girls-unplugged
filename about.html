Our Story

It started with a simple belief.

No girl should feel like she has to figure everything out alone.

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle was founded on 29 September 2025 from a desire to create a space where girls could simply be themselves while learning, connecting and growing with one another.

We believe that growing up can come with questions, uncertainty, big dreams, changing emotions and moments when you just need someone who understands.

Girls Unplugged was created to make room for those conversations — and for the friendships, confidence, courage and growth that can come from them.

A Message From Our Founder

Why I created Girls Unplugged.

A little more about the heart behind the Bloom & Glow Circle.

From Motunrayo

You don't have to grow alone.

I created Girls Unplugged because I know how much it can mean to have a space where you can simply be yourself.

Growing up comes with so many questions. Sometimes you are trying to understand yourself. Sometimes you are figuring out friendships, relationships, school, confidence, your future or the person you want to become. And sometimes, you just need someone to remind you that you are not alone.

I wanted Girls Unplugged to be that kind of space.

Not a space where girls are expected to have everything figured out. Not a space where you have to be the loudest, the most confident or the most accomplished person in the room.

A space where you can come as you are.

When I started Girls Unplugged on 29 September 2025, I did not have everything figured out either. I simply had an idea, a desire to create something meaningful and the belief that girls could benefit from having a sisterhood built around honest conversations, connection, growth and support.

What began as an idea has become a growing circle of girls from different places and backgrounds. Girls who are learning, asking questions, sharing experiences, making friends, discovering themselves and figuring out what it means to become the women they want to be.

And that is what makes Girls Unplugged special to me. It is not about creating a perfect community. It is about creating a genuine one.

I want every girl who enters this circle to know that her voice matters. Her questions matter. Her dreams matter. Her story matters.

I want Girls Unplugged to be a place where girls can find encouragement when they need it, friendships they did not expect, conversations that challenge them, opportunities that help them grow and reminders that they are capable of more than they sometimes believe.

And as this community grows, I want it to remain rooted in the things that matter most to us: respect, safety, growth, sisterhood, voice and possibility.

Girls Unplugged is still growing. We are learning, improving and becoming along the way — just like the girls who make up this circle.

So if you are reading this and wondering whether there is a place for you here, there is.

Come as you are. Bring your voice. Bring your dreams. And bloom with us.

Motunrayo Linda Idowu Founder & Community Director
