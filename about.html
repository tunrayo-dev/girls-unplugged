Our Story

It started with a simple belief.

No girl should feel like she has to figure everything out alone.

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle was founded on 29 September 2025 from a desire to create a space where girls could simply be themselves while learning, connecting and growing with one another.

We believe that growing up can come with questions, uncertainty, big dreams, changing emotions and moments when you just need someone who understands.

Girls Unplugged was created to make room for those conversations — and for the friendships, confidence, courage and growth that can come from them.

A space to be yourself

You do not have to pretend to have everything figured out. Come as you are.

A place to find your people

Meet girls from different backgrounds, places and experiences, and discover the strength of sisterhood.

Why We Exist

Because girls deserve spaces that help them bloom.

We are building a community where girls can grow in confidence, self-awareness, connection and purpose.

Conversations that matter

We create room for honest conversations about growing, relationships, emotions, identity, leadership, dreams and everyday experiences.

Meaningful connection

We bring girls together across backgrounds and borders so they can form friendships, learn from one another and feel less alone.

Growth beyond the moment

We want every girl to leave our spaces with something she can carry into her everyday life.

Room for big dreams

Your ideas, ambitions and dreams belong here. We want girls to see themselves as capable of becoming more.

Our Values

What we believe in.

These values shape how we build our community and how we show up for one another.

Respect

Every girl deserves to be treated with dignity, kindness and consideration.

Safety

We value spaces where girls can speak, share and participate without fear of judgment or humiliation.

Growth

We believe becoming is a process. There is always room to learn, reflect, improve and bloom.

Sisterhood

We believe girls are stronger when they have meaningful connections and support one another.

Voice

Every girl has a voice, a perspective and a story worth hearing.

Possibility

We want girls to imagine more for themselves and feel empowered to pursue what they believe is possible.

What We're Building

A circle that grows with its girls.

Girls Unplugged is more than a place to join. It is a community we are continually building together.

A Safe Community

A space where girls can connect, share experiences, ask questions and feel seen.

Meaningful Experiences

Conversations, activities, games, reflections, learning opportunities and moments that bring girls closer together.

Opportunities to Bloom

Opportunities for girls to develop confidence, leadership, self-awareness and the courage to use their voices.

A Message From Our Founder

You don't have to have it all figured out.

I created Girls Unplugged because I believe girls deserve spaces where they can grow without feeling like they have to do it alone.

I wanted to build a sisterhood where girls can laugh, learn, ask questions, have honest conversations, discover themselves and support one another through the different seasons of becoming.

Girls Unplugged is still growing, and so are we. The goal is not to create a perfect community. It is to create a genuine one — one where girls feel welcome, valued and encouraged to bloom in their own way.

If you're here, I hope you know that there is room for you in this circle.

Come as you are. Bloom with us.

— Motunrayo Linda Idowu
Founder & Community Director
