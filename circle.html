What is the Circle?

Come as you are.

You don't have to be the loudest person in the room. You don't have to have everything figured out. You simply have to be you.

The Bloom & Glow Circle is the community at the heart of Girls Unplugged.

It was created as a space where girls can talk about the things that matter to them, meet people who understand, learn from one another and experience the kind of support that reminds you that you are not alone.

Some days are for deep conversations. Some are for laughing until the chat gets chaotic. Some are for reflecting, learning something new or simply checking in with yourself.

There is room for all of it here.

Real conversations

We create room for honest conversations about growing up, friendships, confidence, school, relationships, dreams and everyday life.

Genuine connection

Meet girls from different places, backgrounds and experiences while building meaningful friendships and connections.

Room to grow

Learn, reflect, ask questions and discover opportunities that can help you grow personally, academically and professionally.

A supportive sisterhood

Celebrate your wins, share your struggles and find encouragement from girls who are also figuring life out.

Life in the Circle

There is always something happening.

From weekly conversations to games, reflections and community calls, the Circle gives us different ways to connect throughout the year.

Honest conversations

We talk about real-life experiences, questions, challenges and the things girls may not always feel comfortable discussing elsewhere.

Wellness & check-ins

We make space to pause, check in with ourselves and remember that taking care of your wellbeing matters.

Fun & games

Because sisterhood should not feel like another assignment. We laugh, play games and create memories together.

Guest conversations

We bring in inspiring guests and facilitators to share knowledge, stories, experiences and practical lessons.

Learning & opportunities

We share useful resources, opportunities and experiences that can help girls explore what is possible for them.

Community calls

Our virtual gatherings give Bloomies a chance to see one another, connect beyond the chat and experience the sisterhood together.

Who is it for?

If you're figuring life out, you're welcome here.

There is no single type of girl who belongs in the Circle. You don't have to fit a certain personality or have everything figured out to be part of this sisterhood.

The curious girl

You have questions, interests and things you want to explore. You are learning more about yourself and the world around you.

The quiet girl

You may not always speak first, but your voice, perspective and experiences still matter.

The growing girl

You're learning, changing and figuring out who you are and who you want to become.

The dreamer

You have dreams that feel big, even if you're still figuring out how to reach them.

The girl who needs community

Sometimes you simply want people around you who will listen, encourage you and remind you that you're not alone.

The girl ready to give back

You want to share what you've learned, support other girls and help make the Circle better for everyone.

Our Community

We protect the space we are building.

A safe sisterhood does not happen by accident. We all play a part in creating a space where people can feel welcome, heard and respected.

Respect everyone

Different opinions, personalities and experiences are welcome. Disrespect is not.

Protect the space

We do not tolerate bullying, harassment, discrimination or behaviour that makes another girl feel unsafe.

Lead with kindness

You never know what someone else may be carrying. Choose empathy, patience and kindness.

Keep conversations thoughtful

Ask questions, listen to others and contribute in ways that help the community grow.

Your seat is here

You don't have to bloom alone.

Come as you are. Bring your voice, your questions, your dreams and your whole self.
