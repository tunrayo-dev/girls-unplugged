Privacy & Trust

Your privacy matters here.

This Privacy Policy explains how Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle approaches information shared through our website and community spaces.

Last updated: September 2026
🌸 Your Privacy Girls Unplugged We believe trust is part of sisterhood.
Our Approach

Privacy should be simple to understand.

We want you to understand what information may be shared with us, why it may be used, and the choices available to you.

A note about this website

Built with simplicity in mind.

Girls Unplugged is currently hosted as a static website. We do not operate a private database through this website itself.

Some links may take you to third-party platforms, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok or Google services, which have their own privacy practices.

Information

What information may be shared?

Different parts of Girls Unplugged involve different kinds of information. Here's what that can look like.

01

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or through a form that opens your email application, you may choose to provide information such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

02

Community information

Information shared when joining or participating in our community may be handled through the platform being used. Please review that platform's privacy settings and policies.

03

Website information

Our website may receive basic technical information through its hosting platform or other services used to operate the site.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through ordinary website browsing.

How We Use Information

Information should have a clear purpose.

When information is shared with us, it should serve a meaningful reason connected to communication, community or the running of the website.

01

To respond to you

When you contact Girls Unplugged, we may use the information you provide to respond to your message or follow up on your enquiry.

02

To support the community

Information shared with us may help us communicate with members, coordinate activities and improve community experiences.

03

To improve our website

Basic website information may help us understand technical issues, improve usability and keep the website functioning.

04

To protect our community

Information may be used when reasonably necessary to address safety, misuse, abuse or other issues affecting the community.

Third-Party Platforms

Some parts of the circle live elsewhere.

Girls Unplugged uses external platforms to communicate, connect and share content. Those platforms may collect and process information according to their own policies.

WhatsApp

Our community uses WhatsApp for group communication and activities.

Instagram & TikTok

We use social platforms to share updates, community moments and public content.

Email

Messages sent to our email address are handled through the email service used to receive them.

Your Choices

You have a say in what you share.

You can decide what information you are comfortable sharing with us and review the settings of platforms you choose to use.

01

Choose what to share

You are not required to provide information simply because you visit our website. Only share information you are comfortable providing.

02

Review platform settings

When you use an external platform connected to Girls Unplugged, you can review its privacy and account settings directly.

03

Ask us questions

If you have a question about information you have shared with Girls Unplugged, you can contact us directly.

Questions?

Privacy is a conversation too.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information shared with Girls Unplugged is handled, reach out to us.
