Girls Unplugged was created from a simple belief: girls should not have to figure everything out alone.

The Bloom & Glow Circle is a space where girls can connect, express themselves, share experiences, discover possibilities and grow together.

How we work

Different strengths, one shared purpose.

A strong community does not depend on everyone doing the same thing. Different strengths, perspectives and skills help the Circle grow.

01

Listen

We pay attention to what girls actually need and make room for honest conversations.

02

Collaborate

Different experiences and skills can come together to create stronger ideas.

03

Create

We turn ideas and conversations into meaningful activities, experiences and opportunities.

04

Grow

We are still learning, improving and becoming better together.

Our culture

The kind of space we want to build.

The way we work together matters because it shapes the experience we create for every Bloomie.

01

Girl-centred

We keep girls at the heart of the community we are building.

02

Intentional

We care about the details and the purpose behind what we create.

03

Open to ideas

Everyone should have room to contribute, ask questions and share ideas.

04

Still growing

We listen, reflect, improve and continue building Girls Unplugged together.
