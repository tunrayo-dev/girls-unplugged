Founder
Founder & Community Director

Motunrayo Linda Idowu

Motunrayo Linda Idowu is the founder of Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle, a growing sisterhood built around connection, self-expression, personal growth and creating meaningful experiences for girls and young women. Driven by the belief that girls should have spaces where they feel seen, heard and supported, she is passionate about turning ideas into initiatives that make a difference.

As Founder and Community Director, Motunrayo leads the vision, direction and development of Girls Unplugged. From shaping community initiatives and creating opportunities for connection to building partnerships and exploring new possibilities for growth, she works to bring the Circle's purpose to life. Her approach to leadership is captured in her personal motto: Action, not titles.

Community & Member Engagement Lead

Fareedah Idowu Bello

Fareedah Idowu Bello is a nursing student at Lagos State University who enjoys learning, exploring new interests and making the most of life's experiences. Her curiosity spans science, healthcare, writing, technology, movies, anime and books. A people-oriented individual, she values kindness, loyalty, family and genuine connections, and enjoys making others laugh while working towards a future where she can support the people she loves.

As Community and Member Engagement Lead at Girls Unplugged, Fareedah's role centres on the people who make the Bloom & Glow Circle special. She contributes to the community's goal of creating an environment where girls can connect, participate and build meaningful relationships. Her interest in people and genuine connections reflects the kind of welcoming sisterhood Girls Unplugged seeks to nurture.

Programs & Activities Lead

Salvation Nneka Ebonne

Salvation Nneka Ebonne is a baker, an avid reader and a curious mind who is rarely without a book, a new recipe to try or a song or story to explore. Her interests include books, music, media and creative work, alongside academic subjects that genuinely capture her attention. Naturally reserved and observant, she appreciates thoughtful conversations and enjoys discovering the person beyond the first impression.

As Programs and Activities Lead at Girls Unplugged, Salvation brings her creativity and thoughtful approach to developing ideas and experiences for the Bloom & Glow Circle. Her role supports the creation of activities that encourage participation, connection and personal growth while allowing members to engage naturally. She brings an appreciation for creativity and fresh perspectives to the community's ongoing development.

Speakers, Partnerships & Communications Lead I

Khadijat Oluwaseun Abdulsalam

Khadijat Oluwaseun Abdulsalam is an Environmental Health student at Lagos State College of Health Technology with a passion for environmental sustainability and community health. She is also interested in educating young girls about sexual integrity, personal growth and menstrual health, reflecting her commitment to subjects that can help girls make informed decisions and build healthier futures.

As Speakers, Partnerships and Communications Lead at Girls Unplugged, Khadijat's role connects with the community's commitment to meaningful conversations, collaboration and creating opportunities for girls to learn. Through this role, she contributes to the work of connecting with speakers, exploring partnerships and supporting communication around the Circle's initiatives. Her interests in health education and girls' development align with the wider purpose of empowering young women.

Content & Social Media Lead

Salimatou Barry

Salimatou Barry is a young woman from The Gambia who is passionate about making a positive difference in people's lives, particularly those of women and girls. She believes in the importance of initiatives that empower young women to discover their potential, develop their leadership abilities and become changemakers in their communities. She is enthusiastic about contributing to organisations and initiatives that share this purpose.

As Content and Social Media Lead at Girls Unplugged, Salimatou's role supports how the Bloom & Glow Circle communicates its message and shares its work with a wider audience. Through content and social media, she contributes to expressing the community's identity, highlighting its initiatives and keeping people connected to its mission. Her passion for women's empowerment reflects the purpose behind the stories Girls Unplugged seeks to tell.
