The people behind the circle

Meet the team behind Girls Unplugged.

Girls Unplugged is built by people who believe girls deserve spaces where they can be themselves, ask questions, build confidence, discover opportunities, and grow together.

Our people

A community built with intention.

Behind every conversation, activity, guest session, opportunity, and community moment is a group of people contributing their time, ideas, skills, and energy to make Girls Unplugged what it is.

We are growing this community carefully, keeping the girls at the heart of what we do and creating room for more people to contribute as the circle grows.

Founder

Motunrayo Linda Idowu

Founder & Community Director

Girls Unplugged started with a simple idea: girls should not have to navigate everything alone.

What began as a small online sisterhood has grown into a community where girls from different parts of Africa can connect, have honest conversations, celebrate one another, discover opportunities, and grow together.

Motunrayo continues to lead the community with a focus on creating meaningful experiences rather than simply building another online group.

“A space does not have to start big to matter. Sometimes, it just has to start.”
