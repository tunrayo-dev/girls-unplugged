Founder & Community Director

Motunrayo Linda Idowu

Founder / Community Director

Girls Unplugged was created from a simple belief: girls should not have to figure everything out alone.

The Bloom & Glow Circle was created as a space where girls can connect, express themselves, share experiences, learn from one another, discover possibilities and grow together.

Motunrayo continues to lead the community with a focus on intentional sisterhood, meaningful experiences and creating room for girls to be seen, heard and supported.

“You don't have to grow alone.”

How we work

Everyone brings something different.

A strong community does not depend on everyone doing the same thing. Different strengths, perspectives and skills help the Circle become more thoughtful and useful.

01

Listen

We pay attention to what girls actually need rather than assuming we already have every answer.

02

Collaborate

We believe ideas become stronger when people bring different experiences and skills together.

03

Create

We turn conversations and ideas into activities, resources, opportunities and experiences girls can use.

04

Grow

We are still learning, improving and becoming better as a community — together.

Our culture

How we show up for each other.

Girls Unplugged is built around the same kind of space we want every girl in our community to experience.

01

Girl-centred

We keep girls at the heart of the decisions we make, the programs we create and the community we build.

02

Intentional

We care about the details. From our conversations to our activities, everything is created with purpose.

03

Open to ideas

Everyone should have room to contribute, ask questions, share ideas and help shape the community.

04

Still growing

We are learning too. We listen, reflect, improve and continue building Girls Unplugged together.

Growing with us

Girls Unplugged is growing, and our team will grow with it. We are building a community where girls can contribute, take initiative and help shape what comes next.
