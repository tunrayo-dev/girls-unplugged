Welcome to Girls Unplugged

A place to be yourself, connect deeply, and grow together.

Girls Unplugged was created from a simple idea: girls deserve spaces where they can talk, laugh, learn, ask questions, express themselves, and grow without feeling like they have to figure everything out alone.

The Bloom & Glow Circle is our online sisterhood — a community built around connection, emotional wellbeing, confidence, leadership, friendship, personal growth, and honest conversations.

It is a space where every girl is encouraged to bring her whole self. The questions, the dreams, the uncertainty, the excitement, the setbacks, the little wins, and everything in between.

Why We Exist

Because growing up can feel easier when you have people beside you.

Girls experience so many transitions while growing into themselves. We believe those experiences deserve spaces for conversation, support, learning and connection.
