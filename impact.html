Our Community

A sisterhood without borders.

Girls Unplugged was created with the belief that a girl shouldn't have to be in the same city, school or country as someone to feel connected to them.

Through our online community, girls from different places can meet, share experiences, support one another and discover that many of the things they are navigating are not theirs to carry alone.

Our community continues to grow organically, one girl inviting another into the circle.

Different places. Shared sisterhood.

Wherever a Bloomie is, she is still part of the circle.

How We Create Impact

It starts with creating spaces girls actually want to be part of.

Our approach is simple: listen, connect, create and keep growing with the community.

We listen

Community conversations help us understand what girls need, care about and want to explore.

We connect

We create opportunities for girls to meet people, exchange ideas and build genuine friendships.

We create

From weekly activities to special events and guest conversations, we turn ideas into experiences.

We keep growing

We learn from what works, listen to feedback and continue improving the community as it evolves.

Our Journey

From one idea to a growing sisterhood.

Girls Unplugged began with a simple desire: to create the kind of space girls could genuinely feel at home in.

September 2025

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle was founded as an online sisterhood for girls to connect, share, laugh, learn and grow together.

Growing the circle

Since then, the community has continued to grow through weekly activities, conversations, games, reflections, guest sessions and opportunities for girls to connect across borders.

And we're only getting started.

The story is still being written — together.
