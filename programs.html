Inside the Circle

Our weekly rhythm.

Some of our most consistent community moments happen through the simple rhythm of the week.

01 MONDAY

Motivation Monday

A little encouragement to help Bloomies start the week with intention and confidence.

Start strong
02 WEDNESDAY

Wellness Wednesday

A chance to pause, check in and talk about wellbeing, self-care and healthy growth.

Check in
03 FRIDAY

Fun Friday

Games, laughter, random conversations and moments to simply enjoy being part of the sisterhood.

Unplug & have fun
04 SATURDAY

Reflection Saturday

A slower moment to reflect on the week, appreciate your progress and think about what comes next.

Reflect & reset
Guest Conversations

Learn from people
with stories to share.

We create opportunities for girls to hear from people with different experiences, skills and perspectives.

These conversations can cover personal growth, leadership, education, technology, wellbeing, careers, creativity and other topics that matter to girls.

The goal is not simply to listen. It is to leave with something you can think about, try or carry with you.

Wellness & Sisterhood

You are a person before you are an achievement.

Growth is not only about what you accomplish. It is also about how you feel, how you relate with others and how you care for yourself along the way.

Emotional wellbeing

Special Events

Sometimes, we do something a little different.

Throughout the year, Girls Unplugged can create special experiences around themes, celebrations, learning, community milestones and important conversations.

Not every community moment has to follow the usual weekly rhythm.

Special events give us room to experiment, celebrate milestones, bring people together and create experiences that Bloomies can remember.

As the community grows, these experiences will continue to evolve with the girls inside it.

How It Works

The community grows with you.

Our activities are designed to remain flexible enough to respond to the interests and needs of the girls in the Circle.

We Listen

We pay attention to the conversations, questions and ideas coming from the community.

We Create

We turn those ideas into conversations, activities, experiences and opportunities where possible.

We Learn

We reflect on what works, what can improve and what the community needs next.

We Grow Together

Girls are not just participants in the Circle. Their experiences help shape what the community becomes.

What's Next
