Let's clear things up.

Here are some of the questions girls and potential collaborators may have about Girls Unplugged.

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is a growing sisterhood for girls and young women to connect, express themselves, learn, grow and bloom together. It is a space for honest conversations, friendships, confidence, emotional wellness, leadership and meaningful connection.

Girls and young women who want to be part of a supportive community are welcome to join. The circle brings together girls from different backgrounds and places, with sisterhood, respect and genuine connection at its heart.

You can join the Bloom & Glow Circle through our WhatsApp community.

Join the WhatsApp Circle

Yes. Joining the Girls Unplugged community is free. Some external opportunities, programmes or events shared through the community may have their own requirements, fees or eligibility conditions, so always check the original information before taking part.

The Circle is built around conversations, connection, fun and growth. Activities can include Motivation Monday, Wellness Wednesday, Fun Friday, Reflection Saturday, game nights, guest conversations, community discussions and special activities.

Explore the Circle

Community activities happen throughout the week, while special events and conversations are scheduled separately. Quarterly community calls and guest conversations are also part of the wider Girls Unplugged experience.
