01 · About Girls Unplugged

The basics

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is a growing sisterhood for girls and young women to connect, express themselves, learn, grow and bloom together.

It is a space for honest conversations, friendships, confidence, wellbeing, leadership, personal growth and simply being yourself.

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle was founded on 29 September 2025.

Girls Unplugged was created from the desire to have a space where girls could feel seen, heard and supported while growing through real life together.

The community is built around connection, honest conversations, confidence, wellbeing, leadership and the freedom to be yourself.

Girls Unplugged was founded by Motunrayo Linda Idowu, who serves as Founder and Community Director.

The community is supported by an admin team responsible for different areas of the Circle.

Bloom & Glow Circle is the name of the sisterhood within Girls Unplugged.

It represents girls growing, discovering themselves, supporting one another and becoming more confident in who they are.

03 · Inside the Circle

What happens here?

The Circle is a space for conversations, encouragement, games, reflections, announcements, opportunities and community moments.

Different activities happen throughout the week, giving members different ways to connect and participate.

Motivation Monday is our weekly community moment for encouragement, positive energy and starting the week with intention.

Wellness Wednesday creates space for conversations and reminders around emotional wellbeing, self-care and healthy growth.

Fun Friday is about relaxing, laughing and connecting. It can include games, girl-talk questions, challenges, anonymous activities and other light-hearted moments.
