01 · About Girls Unplugged

The basics

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is a growing sisterhood for girls and young women to connect, express themselves, learn, grow and bloom together.

It was created as a space for honest conversations, friendships, confidence, emotional wellness, leadership and personal growth.

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle was founded on 29 September 2025.

It was created from the desire to have a space where girls could feel seen, heard and supported while growing through real life together.

The community is built around connection, honest conversations, confidence, wellbeing, leadership and the freedom to simply be yourself.

Girls Unplugged was founded by Motunrayo Linda Idowu, who serves as Founder and Community Director.

The community is supported by an admin team that helps with different areas of the Circle.

“Bloom & Glow Circle” is the name of the sisterhood within Girls Unplugged. It represents girls growing, discovering themselves, supporting one another and becoming who they are meant to be.

03 · Inside the Circle

What happens here?

The Circle is a space for conversations, activities, encouragement, games, reflections, announcements, opportunities and community moments.

Different activities happen at different times, so members can participate in the parts that interest them.

They are recurring community moments built around encouragement and wellbeing.

Motivation Monday focuses on starting the week with encouragement and intention.

Wellness Wednesday creates space for conversations and reminders around emotional wellbeing, self-care and healthy growth.

Fun Friday is about relaxing, laughing and connecting. It can include games and other light-hearted community activities.

Reflection Saturday creates space to slow down, think about the week and consider lessons, feelings, wins and things members may want to improve.

04 · Safety & Community

Keeping the Circle safe

Members are expected to treat one another with respect, protect each other's privacy, avoid harassment and contribute to a welcoming environment.

You can read the full Community Guidelines on The Circle page.

You can share experiences and ask for encouragement, but Girls Unplugged is a peer community and not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, legal or emergency support.

If something is serious or urgent, please reach out to a trusted adult, qualified professional or appropriate emergency service.

You can reach out to a member of the Girls Unplugged admin team or contact us directly so the concern can be addressed.
